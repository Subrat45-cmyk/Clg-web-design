Admissions

We’re glad you’re considering Northstar College. Follow these general steps to get started. Application requirements and dates can vary by program, so contact the college for current details.

  1. Explore your options.

    Review the program areas and identify what interests you.

  2. Check entry requirements.

    Ask the admissions team which qualifications and documents are needed for your chosen program.

  3. Prepare your application.

    Gather your records and complete the application using the instructions provided by the college.

  4. Send your application and follow up.

    Submit it by the relevant deadline and contact admissions if you have questions about what happens next.

Before you apply

  • Confirm the program's entry requirements.
  • Check application dates and any fees.
  • Ask about financial aid, accessibility, or student support if needed.
